How it works

Three steps from property setup to a finished repair.

  1. Add your properties Invite tenants with a share link or QR — they report issues without installing an app.
  2. Route work to vendors Send quote requests, compare responses, and schedule the job.
  3. Track to completion Stay on top of status until the work is done and documented.
